## Needs review

### 2. `services/tree/deadwooding.html` — borderline tree felling mention (carry-forward)
Line ~94: "...this is felling or heavy reduction territory." Does not claim felling as a service, but wording is close to the edge of AGENTS.md's "Never claim tree felling" rule. Consider rewording to make clear felling is outside scope.

### 3. `services/tree/pruning.html` — borderline tree felling mention (carry-forward)
Line ~92: "flag trees that need felling within the year." The statement describes felling as a customer need to arrange elsewhere, but could be tightened.

### 4. `how-to/amelanchier-hedge-kent.html` and `how-to/cotinus-smoke-bush-hedge.html` — broken nav link (carry-forward)
Both pages use an older nav template with `<a href="../services/">Services</a>`. No `services/index.html` exists. All other how-to pages link directly to service sub-pages. These two pages need their nav updated to match the current template.

### 5. YouTube video links — unable to verify (carry-forward)
