## 9. Legal callout consistency — Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981

The reference standard is `when-to-trim-hedges.html`, which explicitly states: "It is an offence to damage a nest that's in active use. Nesting season runs roughly from **1 March to 31 August**."

Articles confirmed with explicit WCA 1981 + specific dates:
- `when-to-trim-hedges.html` ✓
- `wildlife-friendly-trimming.html` ✓
- `laurel-hedge-care.html` ✓ (in FAQ)
- `yew-hedge-care.html` ✓ (in FAQ)
- `choosing-a-hedge-trimmer.html` ✓ (in FAQ)

### ISSUE 3 — `privet-hedge-care.html` — nesting mentioned informally, no Act or dates

The article says "a light trim around mid-June (once nesting's wound down)" and links to when-to-trim-hedges.html in the related guides. But the article body never cites the Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981 or the specific 1 March–31 August window. For an article that discusses trimming timing, an inline callout matching the site convention is missing.

### ISSUE 4 — `leylandii-rules.html` — "When to cut" section has no nesting callout at all

The "When to cut" section recommends "late May and late August" with no mention of nesting season, WCA 1981, or the 1 March–31 August window. The related guides footer links to articles mentioning bird dates but the article itself carries no nesting warning. This is the most significant gap — a page about when to cut conifers says nothing about the legal constraint on summer cutting.

### ISSUE 5 — `renovating-neglected-hedge.html` — references nesting informally, no Act or dates

The article says "no nesting birds" (in context of February/March timing) and "check for nests before anything else" (for conifers). But it never cites WCA 1981 or the specific 1 March–31 August dates. An inline nesting-season callout consistent with the site pattern is missing.

### ISSUE 6 — `trim-a-hedge-straight.html` — says "it's the law" but no Act or dates

Step 1 of the HowTo says "Look for nesting birds or hedgehog trails. If anything's active, wait." The body text says "Don't skip this, it's the law, not a suggestion." But there is no explicit citation of the Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981 or the 1 March–31 August dates. As a how-to aimed at DIY gardeners, this is the one article where a reader most needs the specific legal reference.

Articles that don't require the callout (no timing/trimming focus):
- `composting-hedge-trimmings.html` (clippings disposal, not timing)
- `choosing-hedge-species-kent.html` (species selection)
- `hedge-heights-neighbour-law.html` (High Hedges Act, different legal topic)
