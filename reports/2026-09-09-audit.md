## Needs-review

Items requiring content edits or judgment calls. All are carry-overs. None are new.

### 1. `how-to/when-to-trim-hedges.html` — Missing WCA 1981 callout (HIGH)

The article names the nesting season dates and links to wildlife-friendly-trimming.html but never uses the phrase "Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981". Every other timing-related article on the site includes the statutory name and the 1 March–31 August window with the article-callout pattern. This is the most-trafficked timing article and the omission is the most visible gap. Needs a callout block matching the pattern in `wildlife-friendly-trimming.html` and `when-to-trim-hedges.html` itself. Content edit needed.

### 2. `how-to/salt-tolerant-coastal-hedges-kent.html` — WCA 1981 missing (LOW)

The article delegates nesting season guidance entirely by link with no statutory reference on the page itself. Needs at minimum a one-sentence callout naming the Act and the 1 March–31 August window. Low priority (coastal species guide, not primarily about timing) but inconsistent with house style.

### 3. `how-to/reducing-an-oversized-hedge.html` — Root-relative navigation paths

All nav and footer links use root-relative paths (`/about.html`, `/areas/sandwich.html`, etc.) rather than the relative paths (`../about.html`, `../areas/sandwich.html`) used by every other how-to page. These work correctly when served from the web root but trip up link-checker tools. Carry-over from 2026-09-06; needs a find-and-replace pass on the nav/footer.

### 4. `how-to/box-blight-treatment.html` — Category tag only "species", missing "pests"

The index card has `data-howto-cat="species"`. Box blight is a disease/pest issue and would logically appear under the Pests filter as well. Carry-over; judgment call on taxonomy.

### 5. `how-to/pruning-an-overgrown-shrub-or-bush.html` — Visible tag "Comparison" vs `data-howto-cat="diy"`

The index card shows `<span class="howto-tag">Comparison</span>` (a tag not defined in the filter tabs) but `data-howto-cat="diy"`. The visible label is misleading. Either update the tag text to "DIY" to match the category, or decide on the correct category and update both.

### 6. YouTube video verification — proxy-blocked (carry-over)

Direct HEAD requests to youtube.com are blocked by the outbound proxy. All YouTube links added in this audit session (spiral-topiary-training: `krSavU_qcVc`; pruning-evergreen-hedge-shrubs: `yI1l2YhevaU`; yew-box-ball-care: `kJbDETSMtG8`) and prior sessions could not be verified programmatically. ### 7. `how-to/formal-vs-informal-hedge.html` — Category assignment borderline
