### 2. Possible mis-categorisation — box-blight-treatment.html (data-howto-cat)

**File:** `how-to/box-blight-treatment.html`  
**Issue:** Current `data-howto-cat="species"`. Box blight is a fungal disease treatment guide, not a species care article. Comparable articles use `pests` (hedge-aphids-treatment.html, honey-fungus-hedge.html, box-tree-moth-caterpillar-damage.html). The current value means the article appears in species filtering but not in pests filtering, reducing discoverability for users seeking disease/pest advice.  
**Suggested fix:** Change to `data-howto-cat="pests,species"` — content decision required before touching the index.
