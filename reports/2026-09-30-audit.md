### MEDIUM — 3 broken internal links (persistent, 5th flag)

Files `how-to/august-hedge-care.html`, `how-to/february-hedge-care.html`, and `how-to/january-hedge-care.html` each contain a link to `../hedge-heights-neighbour-law.html`. That resolves to the root directory, not the `how-to/` directory where the file actually lives. The correct href is `hedge-heights-neighbour-law.html` (no `../` prefix). All three are how-to pages linking to another how-to page in the same directory. These are broken on-disk and will 404 in a browser when following that link.

Fix: remove the `../` prefix from those three hrefs.

This article discusses trimming frequency for formal and informal hedges and mentions nesting in a species comparison table, but does not carry the `.article-callout--warn` block referencing the Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981 nesting season (1 March to 31 August). The other timing-style articles (when-to-trim-hedges, wildlife-friendly-trimming, the monthly guides) all carry this callout. Adding it requires new prose matching house style; it is a content decision.
