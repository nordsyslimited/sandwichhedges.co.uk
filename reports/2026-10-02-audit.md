## Needs review

### 1. Three broken relative links (MEDIUM – carry-over, 3rd consecutive flag)

Files:
- `how-to/august-hedge-care.html:278`
- `how-to/february-hedge-care.html:277`
- `how-to/january-hedge-care.html:263`

All three contain: `<a href="../hedge-heights-neighbour-law.html">Hedge height law in Kent</a>`

The `../` prefix resolves one level up from `how-to/` to the site root, where `hedge-heights-neighbour-law.html` does not exist. The correct relative path (same directory) is `hedge-heights-neighbour-law.html`. The fix is a one-character-group deletion. Flagged for the third time; recommend fixing before a fourth audit cycle.

### 2. `formal-vs-informal-hedge.html` missing WCA-1981 nesting callout (LOW – carry-over)

The article mentions trimming timing ("clipped once in August") and nesting value of informal hedges. The August trim recommendation falls inside the nesting-season window (1 March to 31 August under the Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981). The article does not carry the `.article-callout--warn` nesting-season callout that similar timing articles use. Adding the callout requires a prose insert matching house style, so this is a content decision rather than a one-line fix.

### 3. YouTube video embeds unverified (INFO – carry-over)

The HTTPS proxy blocks direct HEAD requests to YouTube. Video link validity cannot be confirmed programmatically from this environment. Approximately 33 embeds are affected. Channel authority for the three most recent articles was verified via web search at time of authorship (2026-10-02 content run).
